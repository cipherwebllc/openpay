お支払い (スキャン・送金) はオフラインではできません。お客様側の通信で お支払いいただく必要があります。店側は受け取り QR の提示のみ可能です。

English

You are offline

Open the receive screen once while online, and you can then show your last receive QR even without a connection.

Paying (scanning / sending) does not work offline — the customer pays over their own connection. The store can only present a receive QR.
